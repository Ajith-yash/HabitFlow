How it works

  1. Add a habit

    Pick a name, schedule and start date.

  2. Check it off

    Mark it complete each day from your dashboard.

  3. Review

    Open Analytics to see what is working.

Why track habits?

Small actions repeated daily add up. Seeing a visible streak makes it easier to keep going, and weekly charts show where a routine needs adjusting.

Local accounts only. Accounts exist only in this browser. No email is sent, nothing is verified online, and accounts cannot sync between devices. Do not reuse an important password.

About HabitFlow

HabitFlow is a simple and user-friendly habit tracking platform designed to help people build consistency, improve productivity and achieve their personal goals. It provides an easy way to track daily routines, monitor progress and understand habits through meaningful visual analytics.

Developer: Ajith Yash

Designed and developed with a focus on simplicity, usability and personal productivity.

Profile & Settings

Profile

Change password

Preferences

Reminders only fire while HabitFlow is open in a browser tab or installed window. They cannot run when it is closed.

Privacy

Accounts, habits and progress are stored in this browser (IndexedDB and LocalStorage). Nothing is sent to any server, and there is no analytics or tracking. Clearing browser data deletes your records, so export backups regularly.
